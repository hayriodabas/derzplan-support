Tile setout · iPhone and iPad

Set out the tiles before you cut.

DerzPlan lays out wall tiling from the sizes you type in. Choose where the first course starts, see which pieces end up as thin slivers, and take the cut list and shopping list to the job.

How it works

What it does

Enter one to four walls in a run: a shower corner, a three-wall shower, a splashback or a single wall. Joints can carry round inside corners, and all walls share one course line. Set the tile size, joint width, the edge gaps of each wall and the pattern: straight, half bond or third bond. Tiles can be rotated.

Drag the green handle in the hatched strip under the wall to move the start line. Hatched areas are start positions that would leave a cut narrower than your sliver limit. “Best start” picks the widest cuts.

Cuts

Identical cut pieces are grouped, with the smallest cut shown against your sliver limit. Tap a count to mark pieces as cut; progress is kept per layout.

Order

Tiled area, whole and cut tiles, extra for breakage, boxes and spare tiles, and an approximate grout powder amount. Every cut piece counts as a whole tile.

Job sheet PDF

A4 or US Letter: drawing to scale with a scale bar, plumb and level marks, order, and a cut list with tick boxes.

Free and Pro

The free version has every feature above, with one active project and one saved layout. DerzPlan Pro is a one-time in-app purchase for unlimited projects and up to 30 saved layouts per project. Nothing renews. The app shows the price in your local currency.

Limits

Walls and tiles are rectangular. Openings and irregular shapes are not drawn. The drawing is to scale on screen but is not for tracing. Check every size on site before cutting.

DerzPlan has no account, no ads and no analytics, and works offline. The app is available in 17 languages; these support pages are in English and Turkish.
