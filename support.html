Support

For help or to report a problem, open a support issue. A free GitHub account is required. Issues are public: do not post customer details, private project names, notes or backups.

Walls, joints and tiles

A project holds one to four walls in a run, such as a shower corner, a three-wall shower, a splashback or a single wall. Joints between walls can carry round inside corners. All walls share one course line, the height of the horizontal joint. Set the tile size, the joint width and the edge gaps (left, right, bottom, top) for each wall. Patterns are straight, half bond and third bond, and tiles can be rotated. Lengths are in millimetres, or in inches with fractions down to 1/16.

Choosing the start line

Drag the green handle in the hatched strip under the wall. Hatched means a start line there leaves a cut narrower than the sliver limit. Nudge with − and + (1 mm or 1/16 in). “Best start” picks the widest cuts. “Other starts” offers Centred, Full tile in the corner and Joint on the reference line. Reference lines (for example a niche, tap or sill) can be added to line the joints up with them.

Reading the Cuts tab

Identical cut pieces are grouped: wall ends cut in width, top and bottom rows cut in height, and corners. The smallest cut is shown against your sliver limit. Tap a count to mark pieces as cut. Progress is kept for each layout. “Show on the wall” highlights those pieces in the drawing.

Reading the Order tab

The tab shows the tiled area, whole and cut tiles, and an extra allowance for breakage (0 to 30 %). Enter tiles per box to get boxes and spare tiles. The grout powder amount is an estimate from joint width and tile thickness; check the bag. Every cut piece counts as a whole tile, because offcuts are never assumed to be reusable. You can share the shopping list.

Job sheet PDF

Open the ••• menu and choose the job sheet. It is made in A4 or US Letter and holds the drawing to scale with a scale bar, plumb and level marks for each wall, the order, a cut list with tick boxes and optional notes. The drawing is to scale on screen, not for tracing.

Saved layouts and undo

Use ••• › Saved layouts to keep alternatives. A layout can keep the measurements it was drawn with. The ••• menu also has Undo last change.

Backups, deleted projects and damaged files

Projects are stored only on your device. Use Settings › Export a backup to save a file where you choose, and Import a backup to bring one in; imported projects are added as copies. Backups are not encrypted. Recently deleted keeps the last 50 deletions. If the projects file is damaged, the app leaves it untouched and asks you to import a backup.

Sound and haptics

Sound and haptics are separate switches in Settings. Sounds follow the silent switch.

DerzPlan Pro and restoring a purchase

Free includes every feature with one active project and one saved layout. Pro is a one-time in-app purchase (non-consumable, nothing renews) for unlimited projects and up to 30 saved layouts per project. It is offered when you start a second project and in Settings › See DerzPlan Pro. To restore, use Restore purchase on that sheet or in Settings, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Restoring needs a network connection. If Pro ends, your existing projects, PDFs and backups stay available.

What DerzPlan does not do

Walls and tiles are rectangular. Openings and irregular shapes are not drawn. Check every size on site before cutting.
