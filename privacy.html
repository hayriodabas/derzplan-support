Privacy

Your notebook stays on your device

DerzPlan stores projects, dimensions, material presets, history and recently deleted work in the app’s local storage. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. It does not send this notebook to the developer.

Backups and sharing are your choice

PDFs and JSON backups are created locally. The iOS Files picker and Share Sheet let you choose a destination or recipient. Backups may include all project names, measurements, notes and retained history. They are not encrypted by DerzPlan. A provider you select, such as iCloud Drive, processes files under its own terms. Device backups controlled by iOS may also include local app data.

Purchases

Apple processes Pro purchases through the App Store. DerzPlan checks signed StoreKit entitlements on the device to determine Pro access. The developer does not receive card or billing details from the app. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Project checkpoints are limited to the latest 30 per project, and the latest 50 deleted projects are retained for recovery. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to backups kept by you or iOS. Exported files and copies stored elsewhere must be deleted from those destinations separately. A damaged notebook can be preserved as a local recovery copy.

Support and this website

Support issues are hosted by GitHub and are public. Do not attach private backups or personal/customer data. The website has no analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical request information to deliver the site; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Contact the developer through the support page.
