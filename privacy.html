Privacy

No data collected

DerzPlan collects no data. It has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. The developer runs no server for the app, and the app uses the network only for the App Store purchase.

Your projects stay with you

Projects are stored on your device. Backups and job sheet PDFs are files you create; they stay on your device or in the place you choose to save or share them, such as Files or iCloud Drive, which handle them under their own terms. Backups are not encrypted. Backups made by iOS may also include the app’s data.

Purchases

DerzPlan Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and purchase restoring; the developer receives no card or billing details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Files you exported must be deleted from where you saved them.

Support and this website

Support issues are hosted by GitHub and are public, so do not post private backups or customer information. This website has no analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical request data to serve the pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement. For help, use the support page.
